## 📖 The Story

> **Leather Shop** isn't just another e-commerce template. It's a testament to the beauty of simplicity — a fully functional online store for premium handmade leather goods, crafted with nothing but **HTML and CSS**. No JavaScript. No frameworks. Just the pure art of web craftsmanship.

Every element, from the elegant typography to the responsive grid, tells a story of precision and attention to detail — much like the leather products it showcases.
